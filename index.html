Privacy Policy — Fun Math Puzzles

Effective date: September 6, 2026
App package: com.amarpy.funmathpuzzles

Fun Math Puzzles ("the app") is made for kids and families to practice mental maths. This policy explains what data we handle.

1. Data we collect: none

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no location tracking.

2. On-device storage only

Your name, scores, XP, badges, and settings are stored only on your device (local app storage) so the game remembers your progress. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted if you uninstall the app or use "Reset all data" in Settings.

3. Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, or location).

4. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for children and contains no chat, no external links, no in-app purchases, and no behavioral advertising. Since we collect no data, there is nothing for parents to request to delete from our servers — device data can be cleared anytime in Settings.

5. Third parties

No third-party services receive any data. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before release.

6. Security

Because all data stays on-device and nothing is transmitted, the risk of remote data exposure is minimal. Keep your device passcode-protected as general practice.

7. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions? Email: teamhandyapps@gmail.com
